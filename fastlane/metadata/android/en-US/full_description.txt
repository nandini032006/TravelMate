TravelMate Beta is a smart mobility planner for Hyderabad, India. Plan multi-modal routes using Metro, MMTS suburban rail, TSRTC buses, and community ride-pooling — all in one app.

Features:
• Multi-modal route planning: Bus, Metro (all 3 lines), MMTS, auto-rickshaw last mile
• Real GTFS data — 1,099+ RTC bus routes processed from official feeds
• Live traffic, weather, and AQI conditions along your route
• Safety analysis with risk zone alerts
• Community RidePool with wallet and points system
• Hyderabad mobility analytics — city-wide transit intelligence heatmap
• Bilingual: English and Telugu (తెలుగు) with 180+ station name translations
• Offline route planning — all computation happens on-device, no backend required
• URL sharing: deep-link journeys with ?from=lat,lon&to=lat,lon
• Accessibility: screen reader support, keyboard navigation, ARIA landmarks

This is a Public Beta release (v0.9.0-beta). Route coverage and analytics continue to improve.
Built with privacy in mind — no account required, no personal data collected.
